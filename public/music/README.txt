Drop your music here (MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, AAC, FLAC, Opus or WebM).
Subfolders are included. Playback support depends on the browser's codecs.
Restart the dev server after adding tracks, or rebuild for a distributed game.
In Settings, use Game music folder to select these tracks, or Choose music folder
for files anywhere on your device. Selected files are copied into browser storage;
reselect the folder to import new or changed files. Nothing is uploaded.
Music plays while any human player is driving or riding as a gunner. Radio speech
pauses it and resumes the same position. Combat and other cues do not stop it.
