The file-by-file source, author, license, edits and SHA-256 inventory is in sources.json. Selected recordings are cropped and converted; foley uses recorded objects and human performances. The wolf and rattlesnake cues are animal recordings. No oscillator/noise-generated SFX or ambience are used. Engine speed changes the playback rate of a recorded loop. Firearm families have six recorded takes each; every event cue has at least three recordings or recorded excerpts. Related cues may share banks. The deer variants are different excerpts of one original call. Playback applies subtle pitch/gain changes, intensity filtering, distance/wall absorption and early reflections. Bike, car, diesel and V8 engines blend recorded running sections by load and RPM. Radio text uses captions by default; the optional browser TTS setting is synthesized speech and does not form part of this asset bank. User-imported music is supplied by the player and is outside this asset license inventory.

Component design uses real recorded material and processing. Electric fan and steam recordings provide cooling-system foley; recorded soft impacts provide flat-tire thumps; metal-sheet impacts provide damaged gearbox and panel foley; filtered car recordings provide drivetrain hum. These are designed component layers, not isolated recordings of every fitted part or engine size. Motor families use bike/car/diesel/V8 recordings, with displacement, layout, load and condition changing pitch and filtering. Alternate component takes include different excerpts of the same recording. Magazine and rack phases include excerpts of recorded airsoft reloads. Gear-shift handling is a recorded shifter, not a recording of transmission internals.
